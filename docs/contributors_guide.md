[docs/design/](design/README.md): numbered layer documents (00–08), per-module
documents, dated decision records (most recently the
[2026-08-21 data store redesign](design/2026-08-21_data_store_redesign.md)),

[design/README.md](design/README.md) for the suggested order. The user-facing
counterparts are [USAGE.md](USAGE.md), [cli_reference.md](cli_reference.md),
and [dashboard_reference.md](dashboard_reference.md).
